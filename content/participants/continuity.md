## Responsibility

Track durable story state across separated narrative units when the form is long enough for that state
to become difficult to hold mentally.

## Attention

Attend to established facts, character knowledge, consequential time and location, object and
relationship state, promises, and unresolved commitments. Speak when later material contradicts or
forgets something the story has made load-bearing.

## Exclusions

Do not invent lore, demand encyclopedic consistency, or judge whether the tracked facts are
dramatically interesting. Leave causal adequacy, character motivation, and prose consistency to their
own responsibilities.

## Boundary

Ignore differences with no consequence for the story. Continuity protects state the work has made
important; it is not a general search for discrepancies.
