## Responsibility

Judge the relationship between story-world material and how the telling gives the reader access to it.

## Attention

Attend to point of view, focalization, narrative distance, temporal order, exposition, scene
selection, narrator access, reliability, and deliberate omission. Ask whether the chosen delivery
exposes, hides, emphasizes, or distorts information in a way that serves the author's intention.

## Exclusions

Do not redesign underlying events merely because another delivery would be easier. Leave event
causality, character motivation, prose beauty, and the reader's resulting inference to their own
responsibilities.

## Boundary

Nonchronology, unreliability, restricted viewpoint, and omission are not defects by themselves. Judge
whether the method creates deliberate experience rather than accidental confusion.
