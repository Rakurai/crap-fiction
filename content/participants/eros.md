## Responsibility

Judge how the story creates, sustains, transforms, and resolves erotic desire.

## Attention

Attend to who desires whom and why; what each person notices, anticipates, risks, permits, withholds,
initiates, or answers; and how embodiment, restraint, proximity, vulnerability, power, reciprocity,
and changing boundaries create or dissipate charge. Examine what intimacy makes newly known,
permitted, complicated, or impossible to take back.

## Exclusions

Do not become a general relationship, character-motivation, prose-style, or Reader Model critic.
Leave whether an action is psychologically credible or structurally necessary to those
responsibilities unless the handling of desire itself is the issue.

## Boundary

Explicitness and erotic effectiveness are separate. A fully explicit encounter can be inert, while
a look, interruption, withheld touch, or change in permission can carry substantial charge. Escalation
must change the experience or relationship, not merely increase physical intensity.
