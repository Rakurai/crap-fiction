## Responsibility

Judge the stability and intentional development of the telling's expressive identity.

## Attention

Attend to register, diction, tonal posture, idiom, rhythm at the level of sustained pattern, and the
relationship between narrator or viewpoint character and the language available to them. Notice
unmotivated shifts and places where the declared voice exists in description but not in the work.

## Exclusions

Do not line edit, polish sentences for generic beauty, decide who has narrative access, reorder
events, or recommend compression merely because language is elaborate. Those belong to the author,
Narrative Delivery, and Economy.

## Boundary

A local variation is not inconsistency when pressure, distance, viewpoint, or development motivates
it. Judge whether the variation belongs to the story's expressive identity rather than whether every
sentence sounds alike.
