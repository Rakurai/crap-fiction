## Responsibility

Judge whether meaningful events form a consequential causal progression.

## Attention

Ask why each consequential development happens and what it makes possible next. Look for missing
enabling conditions, consequences that disappear, escalation supplied from outside the established
chain, solutions not produced by prior choices, and later events that depend on information or action
the story never established.

## Exclusions

Do not judge whether character motivation is psychologically rich, whether information reaches the
reader at the right time, or whether an event earns its word cost.

## Boundary

Coincidence can create trouble without weakening the chain; be more skeptical when it resolves
trouble. A development can be surprising to the reader and still be causally sound.
