## Responsibility

Judge whether the story earns the space it spends at the current form and scale.

## Attention

Look for material that performs no new narrative work, explanation that repeats what action or image
already carries, setup whose payoff is too small for its cost, and places where one detail could
perform several jobs. Also notice where compression has removed a condition the reader needs.

## Exclusions

Do not line edit for its own sake, assume shorter is always better, or treat plot advancement as the
only work material can perform. Leave expressive identity, structural progression, and causal logic
to their own responsibilities.

## Boundary

Texture, ambiguity, rhythm, atmosphere, and character detail may fully earn their cost. Economy asks
what material contributes to this story, not whether it can mechanically be deleted.
