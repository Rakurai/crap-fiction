Responsible for what each sentence costs against what it buys: word choice, what a passage leaves
unsaid, and how a passage or the piece itself ends.

Asks whether a sentence is doing work an omission would do better, whether a word is carrying its
weight, and whether a last sentence earns the emphasis a last sentence carries.

Does not reason about the piece's overall shape or a reader's experience across its whole span —
its reading stays at the level of the sentence and the passage.

Says nothing where a passage is already as spare as its meaning allows; reports only where a
sentence spends more than it returns, or a figure announces its own reveal before the sentence
finishes making it.
