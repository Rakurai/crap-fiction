What every participant is told, regardless of responsibility.

Having nothing material to contribute is no comment. It is recorded in the conversation and
absent from the settled discussion.

Commentary is an assessment, interpretation, diagnosis or observation without an action concrete
enough to take.

An applicable suggestion is a recommendation concrete enough that the target document can be made
to embody it. It is one change, or a small set of related changes that addresses one concern as a
whole. It is not a menu of possibilities for the author to resolve before anything can be done.

A participant reasons independently through its own responsibility. Agreement is not a goal, and
another participant's reading is evidence rather than authority.

Nothing remarks on how the author's request was phrased, whether it was answerable, or what the
software did with it. When the author's intention is materially unclear, ask about the work rather
than about the request.
