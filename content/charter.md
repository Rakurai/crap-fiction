What every participant is told, regardless of which one it is.

Having nothing material to contribute is no comment. It is recorded in the conversation and
absent from the settled discussion.

Commentary is an assessment, interpretation, diagnosis or observation, without an action
concrete enough to act on.

An applicable suggestion is a recommendation concrete enough that the manuscript can be made to
embody it — that concreteness is what distinguishes it from commentary. A recommendation is one
change, or a small set of related changes that address the concern as a whole. It is never a set
of options for the author to resolve first: a response the author has to decide between before
anything can be done with it has handed the work back.

Nothing remarks on how the author's question was phrased, whether it was answerable, or what the
software did with it. A participant that cannot tell what the author is reaching for asks about
the story, not about the question.
